## Q1. What share of global e-commerce sales goes through marketplaces vs. own sites in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Marketplaces dominate: estimates range from ~63.5% (Forrester, online sales) to ~83.4% (ECDB, GMV) of global e-commerce in 2025, and the share is still rising; own-site (1P/D2C) stores hold the remainder (ECDB: ~16.6%). Europe is lower (~61%). In SEA/Vietnam, platform concentration is extreme: Shopee + TikTok Shop = ~97% of Vietnam platform GMV in 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Global share**
- ECDB: marketplaces generated 83.4% of global e-commerce GMV in 2025, up from 81.0% in 2023; 1P online stores' share fell to 16.6% by 2025 — [TyN Magazine (ECDB)](https://tynmagazine.com/marketplaces-now-account-for-83-4-of-global-e-commerce-revenues/); [ECDB blog](https://ecdb.com/blog/marketplace-dominance-in-ecommerce/5194)
- Forrester: online marketplaces account for 63.5% of online sales in 2025 (different definition/methodology than ECDB) — [CS-Cart marketplace statistics](https://www.cs-cart.com/blog/marketplace-statistics/) (secondary)
- Marketplaces = 61% of European e-commerce GMV in 2025 — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/ecommerce/news/marketplaces-account-for-61-of-european-ecommerce-918cd56e-dd3c-4138-96cd-4660455be8e4/)

**Marketplace model — Amazon (economics)**
- Amazon's effective all-in take rate (referral + FBA + advertising) crossed 50% of seller sales in 2022 vs ~1/3 in 2016 (Marketplace Pulse); March 2024 follow-up: typical private-label seller pays 50–60% of sales to Amazon incl. ads — [Fortune 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/02/07/amazon-2024-earnings-third-party-seller-fees-profits/); [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/amazon-all-in-take-rate)
- Direct fees only (referral ~15% + FBA), before ads: ~24–30% of a standard $40 SKU (Eightx analysis) — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/amazon-all-in-take-rate)
- Amazon said it would not raise FBA fees for 2025 (after years of increases) — [Modern Retail, Nov 2024](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/marketplace-briefing-amazon-wont-hike-fees-in-2025-but-some-sellers-say-its-too-little-too-late/)
- Amazon third-party seller services revenue ~US$172.2B in 2025 (+11% YoY); 3P ~62% of unit sales (secondary, unverified) — [Ecom Brainly](https://ecombrainly.com/amazon-seller-statistics/)

**Marketplace model — Southeast Asia**

- (2024 baseline: SEA platform GMV US$128.4B, top 3 = 84%) — [Momentum Works 2024 release](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/press-release-southeast-asias-platform-ecommerce-gmv-reaches-us128-4b-top-3-platforms-increase-market-share-to-84-momentum-works/)
- TikTok Shop + Tokopedia SEA GMV US$45.6B in 2025, up from US$22.6B in 2024 (more than doubled) — [Bisnis.com](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20260516/84/1974139/pangsa-pasar-e-commerce-2025-shopee-tiktok-gerus-bukalapak-blibli-dan-lazada); [DealStreetAsia](https://cdn.dealstreetasia.com/stories/tiktok-shop-gmv-2025-472662)

### Inferences

- In Vietnam, GMV growth (~35–47%) is far outpacing seller count growth (sellers falling), i.e., consolidation toward larger/Mall sellers.

## Q2. What are typical marketplace commission/fee levels and how have they changed?

### Takeaway
Fees are rising everywhere as platforms shift from subsidy to monetization. Amazon's all-in take is 50%+ including ads; in Vietnam, Shopee and TikTok Shop raised fees in multiple rounds (2024, April/May 2025, 2026), with total fee burdens for many sellers now in the mid-teens to ~20%+ of order value before ads, prompting seller exits and regulator intervention in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Vietnam, April 1, 2025: Shopee and TikTok Shop both adjusted fees. Shopee non-Mall sellers: fixed commission raised to an average ~9.5% (max fixed fee from 4% to up to 10%), plus a ~5% payment-processing/transaction fee; some categories (e.g., health/supplements) went from 4% to 9.5% — [VIR](https://vir.com.vn/e-commerce-giants-raise-commission-fees-126579-126579.html); [Vietstock](https://en.vietstock.vn/2025/04/e-commerce-giants-raise-commission-fees-38-609158.htm); [Lao Dong](https://news.laodong.vn/kinh-doanh/nguy-co-nha-ban-hang-roi-bo-khi-shopee-tiktok-shop-tang-phi-1476808.ldo)
- TikTok Shop Vietnam 2025: regular sellers' commission from ~3% to 4% (range 1–4% from 1–3%); Mall sellers from 4.54–5.78% to 6.05–7.7%; further category commission increases from May 9 — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-s-online-sellers-hit-by-shopee-and-tiktok-shop-fee-increases-2380241.html); [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1695086/online-retailers-face-challenges-due-to-increasing-fees.html)
- Platforms' rationale: TikTok cites platform quality/service; Shopee cites investment in payments, processing, seller training and tools; Shopee claimed most sellers would see minimal/no change — sellers disputed this — [VIR](https://vir.com.vn/e-commerce-giants-raise-commission-fees-126579-126579.html)
- Seller pushback/exits: platforms raised fees and cut benefits (e.g., freeship/voucher programs) — [Vietstock, Mar 2025](https://en.vietstock.vn/2025/03/sellers-struggle-as-e-commerce-platforms-increase-fees-cut-benefits-38-604484.htm); ~38,000 shops vanished in Q1 2025 — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/e-commerce-shake-up-as-38-000-shops-vanish-in-vietnam-s-q1-2394489.html); [VOV](https://kynguyenvuonminh.vov.vn/en/economy/sellers-exit-en-masse-from-e-commerce-platforms-in-early-2025-post1218464.vov)
- Metric: active sellers across 4 platforms fell to just over 602,000 (−7% YoY; ~48,000 exited over the year; period unclear) — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/higher-marketplace-fees-weigh-on-online-sellers-2498318.html)
- 2026 round: a May 2026 report says TikTok Shop VN commissions now range ~9%–17.8% across major categories, and Shopee introduced a 1% automatic deduction from ad accounts (unverified against primary fee schedule) — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/ecommerce/news/vietnams-top-ecommerce-platforms-raise-fees-prompting-seller-outcry-1ee5be2b-515a-4f59-bd96-67ed5ec57523/); [Tuoi Tre](https://news.tuoitre.vn/in-vietnam-e-commerce-platforms-race-to-raise-fees-leaving-sellers-in-distress-10326052116130748.htm)
- May 2026: Shopee postponed a new fee policy ("Visibility Maintenance Program") after Vietnam's competition regulator intervened — [The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/vietnam-competition-watchdog-intervenes-shopee-delays-new-fee-policy-d19207.html); calls for regulators to act on fee hikes — [Hanoi Times](https://hanoitimes.vn/regulators-urged-to-take-action-against-online-trading-fee-hikes.644877.html)
- Shopee Malaysia also raised fees (regional pattern) — [Duoke](https://www.duoke.com/en/blog/article/119-Shopee-Malaysia-Fee-Increase-4-Strategies-to-Maintain-Your-Store-Profits)
- Amazon: referral + FBA ≈ 24–30% of a $40 SKU; with ads 50–60% (2024 est.) — [Eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/amazon-all-in-take-rate); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/02/07/amazon-2024-earnings-third-party-seller-fees-profits/)

### Inferences
- Pattern: SEA platforms (Shopee, TikTok Shop) are converging toward Amazon-style monetization: commissions + payment fee + service/program fees + mandatory ad spend. Exact percentages conflict across reports; always check current seller-center schedules.
- Fee pressure pushes small sellers out and toward (a) Mall/brand status, (b) own channels (website, Zalo/Facebook) to protect margin.

### Gaps
- Could not verify full 2026 Shopee/TikTok Shop Vietnam fee tables from primary sources.
- No reliable Lazada/Tiki 2025–26 fee figures found.

## Q3. How big are social/livestream commerce and quick commerce now, and what's the outlook?

### Takeaway
Social/video commerce is the fastest-growing model: ~25% of SEA e-commerce GMV is video commerce (e-Conomy 2025); TikTok Shop global GMV ~US$64B in 2025 (+94%); US social commerce ~US$87B (2025), livestream ~US$15B. China livestream is ~18–25%+ of online retail. Quick commerce is booming in India (~US$11–15B) but loss-heavy, with Blinkit the only profitable player and a shakeout underway; Western q-commerce largely retrenched earlier.

### Cited Findings
**Social / video / livestream (global & US)**
- TikTok Shop 2025 global GMV US$64.3B (+94%) across 16 markets; SEA US$45.6B; Indonesia US$13.1B (2nd-largest market); US US$15.1B (+68% from US$9B) — [Momentum Works US report](https://thelowdown.momentum.asia/new-report-tiktok-shop-u-s-gmv-grew-68-to-reach-us15-1b-in-2025/); [DealStreetAsia](https://cdn.dealstreetasia.com/stories/tiktok-shop-gmv-2025-472662); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/intels/2026-04/29/tiktok-tops-2-billion-users-shop-gmv-climbs-94-in-2025)

- H1 2026 TikTok Shop ~US$50.3B global, US ~US$11.8B (third-party SmartScout estimate) — [SmartScout](https://www.smartscout.com/blog/tiktok-shop-statistics-2026)
- US social commerce (eMarketer via secondary): US$87.02B in 2025 (+21.5%), forecast ~US$101B in 2026; 108.3M US social buyers (47.9% of social users) — [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/social-commerce-statistics-2026)

### Inferences
- Social/livestream: rising — algorithmic discovery + creator affiliate model collapses the funnel; the growth is coming largely from TikTok Shop outside China. In the US, livestream remains small (~14% of TikTok Shop GMV); in SEA/China it is core.

## Q4. What are the economics and current viability of dropshipping and D2C brands (rising CAC, etc.)? Plus cross-border (Temu/Shein), subscription, omnichannel

### Takeaway
D2C own-site infrastructure is thriving (Shopify GMV US$378B in 2025, +29%), but standalone D2C brand economics are squeezed by rising CAC (~+25% YoY reported for 2025) and are pushing brands to omnichannel (marketplaces + retail + TikTok). China-to-US dropshipping and Temu/Shein's ultra-cheap direct-ship model were hit hard by the end of US de minimis (China May 2, 2025; all countries Aug 29, 2025), forcing a shift to local warehouses/local sellers and to Europe. Subscription is mature with high churn; omnichannel/BOPIS keeps growing.

### Cited Findings
**D2C / own website**
- Shopify GMV FY2025 US$378.4B, +29% YoY (2024: US$292.3B; 2023: US$235.9B); Q3 2025 +30% constant currency (~US$92B) — [Shopify 10-K FY2025 (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594805/000159480526000007/shop-20251231.htm); [Shopify 10-Q Q3 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594805/000159480525000089/shop-20250930.htm); [Tiger news](https://www.itiger.com/hant/news/1138314189)
- D2C CAC: +24.7% YoY in 2025 for digital-first DTC brands (paid social/search costs); average e-commerce CAC ~US$68–84 (other source: US$45–70); 69% of DTC brands increasing marketing spend, 76% planning deeper discounts — secondary/unverified — [Ringly DTC stats](https://www.ringly.io/blog/dtc-ecommerce-statistics-2026); [Swell](https://www.swell.is/content/dtc-ecommerce-statistics)

**Cross-border (Temu/Shein)**
- Temu + Shein likely >30% of daily US de minimis packages (2023 congressional report) — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/shein-temu-aliexpress-end-of-customs-exemption-in-the-us-reshuffles-cards/2025050681456)
- After de minimis end for China (May 2, 2025): Shein sales −16% to −41%, Temu up to −32% in following days (Bloomberg Second Measure) — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/business/shein-temu-aliexpress-end-of-customs-exemption-in-the-us-reshuffles-cards/2025050681456); Shein US sales −8% YoY in Sept 2025 after global de minimis end Aug 29 — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/shein-us-sales-drop-after-de-minimis-loophole-ends)

## Q5. How big is B2B e-commerce and how is it done?

### Takeaway
B2B e-commerce is the largest e-commerce segment by value: US B2B e-commerce site/portal sales ~US$2.3T in 2024 (eMarketer, via secondary), growing ~8–10%/yr; global estimates range US$11–33T depending on whether EDI and e-procurement are counted. It's done via supplier websites/portals, EDI, e-procurement networks, and B2B marketplaces (Amazon Business, Alibaba.com).

### Cited Findings
- Forrester (2022 forecast): US B2B e-commerce US$1.7T in 2021 (16% of US B2B sales) → US$3T by 2027 (24%), 10.7% CAGR — [Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-us-b2b-e-commerce-will-reach-an-estimated-3-trillion-by-2027) (older data, 2022)
- eMarketer (via secondary): US B2B e-commerce site sales US$2.297T in 2024 (+10.5%), projected US$3.027T by 2028 (7.8% CAGR); B2B e-commerce taking a growing share of US B2B sales — [eLogic](https://elogic.co/blog/b2b-ecommerce-statistics/); [eMarketer chart](https://www.emarketer.com/chart/c/358367/b2b-ecommerce-will-take-increasingly-larger-share-of-us-b2b-sales-358367)
- Global B2B e-commerce estimates range US$11T–US$33T depending on inclusion of EDI, e-procurement portals vs. web-only orders — [eLogic](https://elogic.co/blog/b2b-ecommerce-statistics/)
- Total US B2B sales ~US$15T in 2025 (Dreamdata-sourced, treat cautiously) — [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/b2b-growth/news/us-b2b-sales-top-15-trillion-ecommerce-redefines-how-companies-buy-c1894778-f7f8-46f4-b25e-6811c6969db8)

### Inferences
- Channels: (1) EDI (largest by value, legacy), (2) supplier self-service portals/websites (fastest-growing), (3) e-procurement networks (SAP Ariba, Coupa), (4) B2B marketplaces (Amazon Business, Alibaba.com, and in SEA/VN wholesale platforms). Economics favor lower take rates, account-based pricing, credit terms — knowledge-based, not sourced in this round.

## Summary of rising vs declining models (synthesis)
- Rising: video/social commerce & livestream (TikTok Shop +94% global, ~25% of SEA GMV, 41% of VN platform GMV); marketplaces overall (share rising to 63–83%); omnichannel/BOPIS (+16% US); B2B digital ordering; quick commerce in India (but profitability shakeout).
- Under pressure / declining: China-to-US direct-ship cross-border (Temu/Shein after de minimis end); classic AliExpress dropshipping; small sellers on SEA marketplaces (fee hikes, exits); pure paid-social D2C brands (CAC inflation); Lazada/Tiki share in Vietnam; Western rapid-delivery startups.
