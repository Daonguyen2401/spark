## 1. Market size, growth and platform shares (GMV 2025, 2026 forecast; Metric.vn, YouNet ECI, e-Conomy SEA, MoIT)

### Takeaway
Vietnam's B2C e-commerce reached about US$25bn (e-Conomy SEA, GMV) to US$31–32bn (MoIT retail e-commerce) in 2025. Metric's 4-marketplace tracker shows 429.7 trillion VND (+34.75%). The market is now a Shopee–TikTok Shop duopoly (~97% of the 4 tracked marketplaces). TikTok Shop's share jumped from ~30% to ~41% in 2025 and by Q3 2026 was nearly level with Shopee. Growth is still strong in 2026: Q3 2026 was up ~45% YoY on Metric's numbers.

### Inferences
- Implied full-year 2026 (Metric 4 marketplaces): about 442 + 165.5 ≈ **~607 trillion VND (~US$23bn)**, roughly +40% vs 2025. This is my arithmetic from the 9M figure and Metric's Q4 forecast, not a published number.
- By Q3 2026, TikTok Shop is at ~48–49% of the Shopee+TikTok pair on YouNet's data, so a share crossover in 2026–2027 is plausible. Note also that TikTok has more active sellers than Shopee.
- Lazada and Tiki are marginal (~3% combined). A new seller should treat them as optional channels at most.
- The number of active shops is flat or falling while GMV rises 35–45%. That means concentration: GMV per shop is rising and the long tail is being squeezed out by fees, tax compliance and competition.

## 2. Marketplace fees and seller conditions 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Fees rose sharply again in 2025–2026. On Shopee, the payment fee became a "transaction processing fee" at **6%** (including COD orders, previously ~4.91%). Fixed/commission fees run ~8.8–11.7% in major categories for non-Mall sellers. A new Voucher Xtra service fee of 5.5% (capped at 50k VND per item) applies from 23/05/2026. TikTok Shop also raised commissions in May 2026. The National Competition Commission intervened to ask Shopee not to apply the new fees. With withholding tax (1.5%) and affiliate commissions on top, total take-rate for a small seller commonly runs 20–30%+ of the sale price.

### Cited Findings
- Shopee renamed the payment fee to a "transaction processing fee" (from 24/04/2026). From May 2026 it is **6%** for non-Mall sellers, and 6% for Mall sellers from 08/05/2026. COD orders went from 4.91% to 6% (VAT included) — [MISA eShop](https://www.misaeshop.vn/24423/phi-san-shopee/); [Sapo – fee update May 2026](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/cap-nhat-phi-san-tmdt-thang-5-2026)
- Shopee fixed (commission) fees for non-Mall sellers in Fashion, FMCG and Home & Living are commonly **8.8%–11.7%** (period 01/05–01/07/2026). Fees were updated across many categories from 23/05/2026, with a new Mall-specific schedule from 29/05/2026. Mom & Baby was cut (e.g., 11.5% → 10.5%) — [MISA eShop](https://www.misaeshop.vn/24423/phi-san-shopee/); [Sapo – Shopee tăng phí từ 23/05](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/shopee-tang-phi-hang-loat-tu-23-05)
- **Voucher Xtra service fee: 5.5% per item, max 50,000 VND/item**, applied to all categories from 23/05/2026 — [Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/shopee-tang-phi-hang-loat-tu-23-05)
- In mid-May 2026, Shopee and TikTok Shop simultaneously announced higher fixed fees or commissions for some categories — [VnBusiness](https://vnbusiness.vn/shopee-tiktok-shop-dong-loat-tang-phi-hoa-hong.html); [Doanh nghiệp & Hội nhập](https://doanhnghiephoinhap.vn/nguoi-ban-gap-kho-khi-cac-san-thuong-mai-dien-tu-dong-loat-tang-phi-136711.html); [Thuế Hải quan – "áp lực bủa vây người bán nhỏ lẻ"](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/san-tang-phi-ap-luc-bua-vay-nguoi-ban-nho-le-133729.html)
- The **National Competition Commission asked Shopee not to apply the new fees** to sellers — [Viettimes](https://viettimes.vn/uy-ban-canh-tranh-quoc-gia-yeu-cau-shopee-khong-ap-dung-cac-loai-phi-moi-post200946.html); [Báo Đầu tư](https://baodautu.vn/yeu-cau-shopee-khong-ap-cac-loai-phi-moi-voi-nguoi-ban-hang-tren-nen-tang-d606848.html). The outcome of this intervention (whether Shopee rolled back, delayed or kept the fees) was not confirmed in search results.
- Market consolidation and seller exits: >600k shops quit in 2025, and Shopee's growth slowed — [Doanh nhân/BPL](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/hon-600-000-shop-chia-tay-thi-truong-thuong-mai-dien-tu-trong-nam-2025-da-tang-cua-shopee-cham-lai.html); "80,000 shops sold zero orders" paradox — [VnReview](https://vnreview.vn/threads/nghich-ly-thuong-mai-dien-tu-viet-nam-doanh-thu-tang-80-000-gian-hang-bo-cuoc.65752/latest)
- VCCI overview of what sellers pay on marketplaces — [VCCI](https://vcci.com.vn/tin-tuc/kinh-doanh-tren-san-thuong-mai-dien-tu-chiu-phi-ra-sao)

### Inferences
- Rough Shopee non-Mall stack on a fashion item: ~10% fixed + 6% transaction + up to 5.5% Voucher Xtra (if enrolled) + other programs (Freeship Xtra, ads) + 1.5% tax withholding if applicable. That is easily **~20–25% before ads and affiliate commission**. TikTok Shop sellers add 10–20%+ creator commission on affiliate-driven sales. This is my estimate; specific Freeship Xtra and TikTok commission rates were not verified.
- Regulatory pushback (the Competition Commission) means fee policy is now partially politically constrained. Sellers should still budget for further increases.

### Gaps
- Precise current TikTok Shop Vietnam commission/transaction fee schedule (2026) was not retrieved.
- Lazada/Tiki 2026 fee changes were not researched due to their low share.
- Final outcome of the Competition Commission vs Shopee fee dispute is unknown.

## 3. Livestream, affiliate and KOC selling

### Takeaway
Video and livestream commerce drives market growth. Vietnam is SEA's #2 video-commerce market (1.3bn transactions in 2025, +60%), TikTok Shop's share rose to ~41%, affiliate livestream creators doubled in 2025, and roughly 60% of TikTok Shop seller revenue is reported to come via affiliates. From 1/7/2026, livestream sellers face formal legal duties under the E-commerce Law.

### Cited Findings
- Vietnam is SEA's 2nd largest video-commerce market: 1.3bn transactions, +60% YoY (e-Conomy SEA 2025) — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-s-digital-economy-hits-usd-39-billion-ai-and-e-commerce-drive-growth-2466540.html)
- TikTok Shop 2025: GMV +66%, affiliate livestream creators x2, short videos with shopping carts +45% — [Brands Vietnam](https://www.brandsvietnam.com/congdong/topic/tiktok-shop-cong-bo-tang-truong-66-nam-2025-va-dinh-huong-giai-doan-phat-trien-moi-tai-viet-nam)
- ~60% of TikTok Shop seller revenue comes via affiliates (the timing and base of the cited "US$5bn GMV / US$300m affiliate" figure are unclear) — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/techconnect/thi-truong-affiliate-marketing-tang-truong-nong-tren-cac-san-thuong-mai-dien-tu-shopee-tiktok-shop.htm)

## 4. Logistics, delivery, payments, returns

### Takeaway
Last-mile delivery is now platform-captive. Shopee's SPX (~35% share by BSC's estimate) and TikTok-favoured J&T (~14%) lead marketplace parcels, while legacy players (Viettel Post, GHTK, GHN) lose platform volume. Ninja Van exited Vietnamese parcel delivery in September 2025. Payments are shifting fast to bank-transfer/QR and e-wallets, but credible 2025 COD-share figures conflict widely (16% to ~67%). Platform-wide return and failed-delivery rates were not found.

### Cited Findings
**Delivery**
- BSC (late 2025): **SPX Express 34.8%** and **J&T Express 14.0%** market share. Traditional carriers (Viettel Post, GHTK, GHN) are losing share as marketplaces prioritize in-house or partner carriers — [BSC report](https://new.bsc.com.vn/Report/ReportFile/12766691)
- Vietdata (Sept 2025, metric unspecified): Viettel Post 17.2%, SPX 15.7%, GHTK 14.5%, VNPost 13.8%, J&T 10.6%, GHN 7.9%, EMS 3.1% — reported via search summary of [Diễn đàn Doanh nghiệp](https://diendandoanhnghiep.vn/mieng-banh-thi-truong-chuyen-phat-nhanh-viet-nam-10150141.html). This conflicts with BSC; it is probably revenue-based or for the whole CEP market rather than e-commerce parcels.
- Viettel Post Q1 2026 revenue was 4,758bn VND (−5.6% YoY). Carriers are pivoting to end-to-end logistics because marketplace volume "is no longer a support pillar" — [Nhà đầu tư](https://nhadautu.vn/khi-nguon-hang-tmdt-khong-con-la-be-do-cua-doanh-nghiep-chuyen-phat-d105447.html)
- **Ninja Van exited Vietnam's express delivery (B2B and B2C)**. It stopped taking new orders in early September 2025 (dates reported as 3/9, 8/9 or service end 30/9/2025) and is refocusing on B2B and cross-border — [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1724651/ninja-van-to-exit-viet-nam-delivery-scene-amid-intense-competiton.html); [Báo Đầu tư](https://baodautu.vn/ninja-van-lang-le-khep-lai-hanh-trinh-d387110.html); [VCCI](https://vcci.com.vn/tin-tuc/logistics-thuong-mai-dien-tu-viet-nam-buoc-vao-giai-doan-thanh-loc)
- Most postal operators reported strong on-time performance in early 2026 (MIC/Ministry inspection) — [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1764042/most-postal-services-operators-record-strong-on-time-delivery-performance.html)
- Regionally, J&T, SPX and Lazada Logistics handle more than two-thirds of SEA e-commerce logistics (22.3bn parcels) — reported in [CafeF (J&T tag)](https://cafef.vn/jt.html)

**Payments**
- COD share estimates conflict: "over 80%" (Allied Market Research 2022 **[older]**); ~67% of online purchases (PCMI 2024 **[older]**); a commercial market page claims 2025 split of **e-wallets 38%, bank transfer 28%, COD 16%** — [Antom (Ant Group) Vietnam market page](https://www.antom.com/markets/vietnam); trend analysis of the shift from COD to QR, e-wallets and instant transfer for 2026–2030 — [Tạp chí Kinh tế Tài chính (research)](https://nghiencuu.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/thanh-toan-trong-thuong-mai-dien-tu-tai-viet-nam-xu-huong-chuyen-dich-thuan-loi-rui-ro-va-ham-y-chinh-sach-huong-toi-giai-doan-2026-2030-131998.html)
- Shopee now charges the same 6% transaction fee on COD as on other methods, up from 4.91% — [MISA eShop](https://www.misaeshop.vn/24423/phi-san-shopee/)

### Inferences
- The Antom 16% COD figure probably reflects payment *method by value* among digitally native shoppers and looks low. Older surveys at 60–80% measure *share of buyers who use COD*. The safest framing: COD is still widely used, especially outside big cities and on TikTok Shop, but VietQR/bank transfer and e-wallets (MoMo, ZaloPay, ShopeePay) are growing fast.
- With SPX and J&T dominant on platform orders, sellers have little carrier choice on Shopee and TikTok. Carrier choice (GHN, GHTK, Viettel Post) matters mainly for the seller's own website/Facebook/Zalo orders.

## 5. Regulation affecting online sellers (tax, E-commerce Law, livestream, cross-border/Temu/Shein, low-value imports)

### Takeaway
Four big changes. (1) Since **1/7/2025 (Decree 117/2025/ND-CP)**, platforms with payment functions withhold VAT+PIT on each sale by household and individual sellers. (2) From **1/1/2026**, presumptive "lump-sum" tax (thuế khoán) and the business license fee are abolished, and the tax-free threshold rose to **500m VND/year** (Resolution 198/2025/QH15; Decree 68/2026/ND-CP of 5/3/2026). (3) The new **E-commerce Law No. 122/2025/QH15** (passed 10/12/2025, effective **1/7/2026**) regulates livestream sellers, requires identity verification, and tightens rules on foreign platforms. (4) The low-value import VAT exemption (<1m VND express parcels) was **abolished from 18/2/2025**, and Temu (and Shein) have been suspended or unregistered since late 2024.

### Cited Findings
**Platform tax withholding – Decree 117/2025/ND-CP**
- Effective 1/7/2025: domestic and foreign platform operators with payment functions withhold and pay VAT and PIT for household and individual sellers when each transaction is confirmed and paid. Sellers then do not need to re-declare that revenue. Platforms without payment functions must periodically report seller info to tax authorities — [MISA AMIS](https://amis.misa.vn/228691/moi-nghi-dinh-117-2025/); [VCCI](https://vcci.com.vn/tin-tuc/san-giao-dich-thuong-mai-dien-tu-nop-thue-thay-cho-ho-kinh-doanh-tu-ngay-1-7); [Luật Việt An](https://luatvietan.vn/thue-kinh-doanh-thuong-mai-dien-tu-cua-ho-ca-nhan-theo-nghi-dinh-117-2025-nd-cp.html); [Nhân Dân](https://nhandan.vn/quan-ly-thue-voi-kinh-doanh-online-khong-that-thu-khong-thu-chong-thu-post888124.html)
- Withholding rate for goods: **1.5% of revenue (1% VAT + 0.5% PIT)**. An example states Lazada withholds 1.5% per transaction — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/kien-thuc/cach-tinh-thue-ban-hang-online/). Note: the 1%/0.5% split for goods is the standard household-business rate, but the search summary did not explicitly confirm it for 2026.
- Sellers may authorize platforms to issue e-invoices (Decree 117 together with Decree 70/2025) — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/san-da-nop-thue-thay-ho-kinh-doanh-online-co-can-ke-khai-va-phat-hanh-hoa-don.htm)
- **63 platforms** are filing and paying tax on behalf of sellers under Decree 117 (infographic, mid-2026) — [Thuế Hải quan](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/infographic-da-co-63-san-tmdt-khai-nop-thue-thay-theo-nghi-dinh-1172025nd-cp-102519.html)
- One source (meinvoice) cites a different date (06/04/2026) for platform obligations. That probably refers to a later amending decree or guidance and is unverified — [meinvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/35149/nghi-dinh-117-2025-nd-cp-ve-thue-thuong-mai-dien-tu/)

**Household business tax reform from 2026**
- Lump-sum tax abolished from 1/1/2026 (Resolution 198/2025/QH15). The business license fee (lệ phí môn bài) ended 1/1/2026. Threshold: revenue **≤500m VND/year** is exempt from VAT and PIT (previously 100m) — [Thư viện Pháp luật](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/ma-so-thue/phap-luat-thue/tong-hop-nhung-chinh-sach-thue-co-hieu-luc-tu-ngay-01012026-217883.html); [einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/hkd-tinh-thue-khi-bo-thue-khoan); [Luật Việt An](https://luatvietan.vn/cach-tinh-thue-ho-kinh-doanh-2026-khi-bo-thue-khoan-sang-ke-khai-va-tu-nop-thue.html)
- **Decree 68/2026/ND-CP (5/3/2026)** on taxation of household and individual businesses. Article 11 covers declaration and withholding for sellers on e-commerce platforms. Above the 500m threshold, platforms withhold — [Nhà đầu tư](https://nhadautu.vn/chu-quan-san-thuong-mai-dien-tu-khau-tru-nop-thue-thay-nguoi-ban-d103320.html); [meinvoice – ND 68/2026](https://meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/44997/nghi-dinh-68-2026-nd-cp-ve-thue-ho-kinh-doanh/); [Thuế Hải quan infographic – online-sales tax rates from 2026](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/infographic-muc-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-ban-hang-online-tu-nam-2026-142474.html)
- Conflicting interpretations exist. VAT over the threshold applies to *total* revenue, while PIT applies only to the *excess* over 500m. Reported PIT methods differ (0.5% of excess revenue vs 15–20%/17% on profit for larger households) — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/kien-thuc/thue-kinh-doanh-online/); [einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/hkd-tinh-thue-khi-bo-thue-khoan). For 2026, filings are due twice (31/7/2026 and 31/1/2027) — [Kế toán Thuận Thiên](https://ketoanthuanthien.vn/hoi-dap/ho-kinh-doanh-con-duoc-ap-dung-thue-khoan-khong/)
- Policy aim: 100% of online goods sales covered by e-invoices — [VietnamFinance](https://vietnamfinance.vn/muc-tieu-100-mua-ban-hang-hoa-online-co-hoa-don-dien-tu-d128239.html)

Reported as 7 chapters and 41–42 articles — [Thư viện Pháp luật (full text)](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thuong-mai/Luat-Thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025-so-122-2025-QH15-662035.aspx?tab=3); [Tạp chí Công Thương](https://tapchicongthuong.vn/luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-chinh-thuc-duoc-quoc-hoi-thong-qua-349006.htm); [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/luat-thuong-mai-dien-tu-2025-cam-cuong-va-minh-bach-hoa-thi-truong.htm)

### Inferences
- Since Decree 68/2026 exempts ≤500m VND/yr, many micro-sellers below the threshold should no longer be withheld on, or can claim back. But how platforms handle sellers below the threshold (e.g., withholding until the threshold is crossed vs. not at all) was not confirmed. A seller should check each platform's 2026 tax notice.
- The Law's identity verification plus platform withholding makes anonymous or "grey" selling nearly impossible on major platforms from mid-2026. Compliance (registered household business, product origin papers, e-invoices) is now a prerequisite, and it favours sellers with proper sourcing.
- Temu/Shein restrictions and the end of the low-value VAT exemption somewhat protect domestic sellers from ultra-cheap Chinese direct imports. Cross-border goods still flow through Shopee/TikTok "global" sellers, though.

### Gaps
- Full text of the seven Article 24 livestream duties could not be fetched. The implementing decree(s) for Law 122/2025 (penalties, KOL liability details) were not found.
- Current (Nov 2026) registration status of Temu and Shein was not confirmed.
- Whether platforms withhold for sellers under 500m in 2026, and the refund process for over-withheld tax, are unclear.
- Rules on livestreamer *advertising* liability (Advertising Law amendments 2025) were not researched.

## 6. Practical implications for a seller starting today (Nov 2026) — synthesized

### Takeaway
Start on **TikTok Shop + Shopee** (≈97% of marketplace GMV). Build a creator/affiliate engine from day one. Price for a ~20–30% platform take plus ads. Register as a household business (or company) with clean sourcing papers, because identity verification, platform tax withholding and e-invoicing are now enforced.

### Inferences
- **Channel:** TikTok Shop for discovery (especially fashion, beauty and impulse FMCG). Shopee for search-led and higher-ticket categories (phones, appliances, home), where Shopee still leads. Lazada/Tiki are optional.
- **Unit economics:** Model at least 25–35% of sale price for platform fees, vouchers, tax and affiliate commission, plus 5–15% ads. Avoid low-margin commodity items where cross-border sellers compete.
- **Compliance checklist:** register a household business or company; complete eKYC/identity verification for any livestream host; keep invoices and origin papers for products (the Law tightens counterfeit and consumer-protection enforcement); authorize the platform for e-invoicing; track annual revenue against the 500m VND threshold.
- **Logistics/payments:** platform orders ship via SPX (Shopee) or J&T and partners (TikTok). For off-platform orders (Facebook, Zalo, website), use GHN/GHTK/Viettel Post and push VietQR prepayment to cut COD failure risk. Ninja Van is no longer an option.
- **Content engine:** recruit KOCs early. The doubling of affiliate creators in 2025 means supply exists, but legal liability from 7/2026 will push KOCs toward compliant brands.

- No verified data on return rates, typical ad cost-of-sale (ACOS/ROAS), or KOC commission benchmarks to calibrate unit economics precisely.
